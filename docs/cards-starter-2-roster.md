# Starter 2 Card Roster

Starter 2 expands the baseline play patterns from Starter 1 with a full-lane
payoff, a global Ongoing buff, durable board presence, prediction, and targeted
ally improvement. It also introduces named story characters, starting with Mo
Kandu, and the Black Table through its emissary, the Harbinger.
