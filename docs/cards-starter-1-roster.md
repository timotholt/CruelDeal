# Starter 1 Card Roster

Starter 1 is the introductory 13-card collection. It teaches baseline power
rates, lane commitment, prediction, card generation, center-lane placement,
and a single lane-scaling payoff. It deliberately does not introduce engine
archetypes such as Destroy, Discard, or Move.

## Notes

- This is a locked design roster, not a declaration that every listed card is
  already implemented in the active manifest.
- Existing live cards retain their current mechanics unless explicitly changed
  in a separate implementation task.
- The collection-progression labels are: `unassigned`, `starter-1`,
  `starter-2`, `starter-3`, and `pool-1` through `pool-5`.
