CYBERPUNK CCG — LAUNCH ARCHETYPE PACKAGES v0.2
Plain-text design spec
Created: 2026-04-23
Updated: 2026-04-23 — persistent card entities, zone-change model, banish rule

============================================================
0. PURPOSE
============================================================

This file defines the launch archetype package structure.

This is NOT a card list.

This is the step before card design.

Each archetype should be built as a small package of functional slots:

    Enablers
    Payoffs
    Finishers
    Bridge cards
    Counters
    Beginner cards
    Advanced cards

A 100+ card launch set should not contain 100 isolated ideas.
It should contain overlapping packages built from tracked game variables.


============================================================
0A. ENGINE MODEL CORRECTION
============================================================

Cards are persistent entities.

In this game engine, Destroy, Discard, and Banish do not delete cards from the
system. They move cards to zones:

    Destroy:
        move card to destroyed pile / death pile

    Discard:
        move card to discard pile

    Banish:
        move card to banish pile

The card object remains available to the engine through current_zone,
zone_history, and zone-change records.

Design rule:

    Destroy pile and discard pile may have recovery mechanics.
    Banish pile must not have recovery mechanics.

Allowed:
    Count cards in the banish pile.
    Trigger when cards are banished.
    Care that cards were banished this game.

Forbidden:
    Pull from banish.
    Recover from banish.
    Regenerate from banish.
    Copy from banish as a workaround for recovery.

============================================================
1. PACKAGE SLOT DEFINITIONS
============================================================

------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Enabler
------------------------------------------------------------

Purpose:
    Starts the archetype's core loop.

Example function:
    Destroy another card.
    Create a card.
    Draw a tagged card.
    Leave Energy unspent.

Card design question:
    What card lets the deck do its thing?

------------------------------------------------------------
1.2 Payoff
------------------------------------------------------------

Purpose:
    Rewards the archetype for doing its core action.

Example function:
    Gain Power for destroyed cards.
    Buff created cards.
    Reduce Cost after unspent Energy.
    Buff cards in full lanes.

Card design question:
    Why does the player care about repeating the loop?

------------------------------------------------------------
1.3 Finisher
------------------------------------------------------------

Purpose:
    Provides a late-game swing or win condition.

Example function:
    Big Power scaling.
    Final turn copy.
    lane-wide buff.
    large discounted body.

Card design question:
    How does the deck close the game?

------------------------------------------------------------
1.4 Bridge Card
------------------------------------------------------------

Purpose:
    Connects two archetypes without belonging purely to either.

Example:
    A created card that destroys itself.
    A Drone that is good in Swarm and Destroy.
    An AI card that buffs Ongoing and created cards.

Card design question:
    What other deck would want this card?

------------------------------------------------------------
1.5 Counter Card
------------------------------------------------------------

Purpose:
    Gives opponents interaction.

Example:
    Remove Ongoing text.
    Punish created cards.
    Stop On Reveal abilities.
    Reset modified Power.

Card design question:
    What keeps this archetype from becoming oppressive?

------------------------------------------------------------
1.6 Beginner Card
------------------------------------------------------------

Purpose:
    Teaches the archetype with very simple text.

Example:
    When Destroyed: Gain +2 Power.
    Ongoing: Your other cards here have +1 Power.

Card design question:
    Can a new player understand the archetype from this card?

------------------------------------------------------------
1.7 Advanced Card
------------------------------------------------------------

Purpose:
    Adds skill expression.

Example:
    Timing-dependent payoff.
    copied text with constraint.
    energy restraint payoff.
    targeted draw.

Card design question:
    What rewards mastery without requiring long text?

============================================================
3. ARCHETYPE: DESTROY
============================================================

Core domain:
    destroyed cards

Secondary domains:
    lane targeting
    cost reduction
    cards with When Destroyed text

Mechanical identity:
    Cards become Power.

Player question:
    What should I sacrifice?

Primary timing/action words:
    On Reveal
    When Destroyed
    Destroy
    Gain Power
    Costs less

------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Package Slots
------------------------------------------------------------

Recommended launch slots:
    2 basic destroy enablers
    2 When Destroyed value cards
    2 destroyed-count payoffs
    1 big finisher
    1 bridge to Corporate Copy
    1 bridge to Black Market
    1 beginner card
    1 counter/anti-destroy card

Total:
    10-11 cards

------------------------------------------------------------
3.2 Required Design Functions
------------------------------------------------------------

Enabler A:
    Low-cost card that destroys another friendly card.

Enabler B:
    Mid-cost card that destroys based on visible lane condition.

Value Card A:
    Card that rewards being destroyed with Power.

Value Card B:
    Card that rewards being destroyed with card advantage or relocation.

Payoff A:
    Card that grows when your cards are destroyed.

Payoff B:
    Card that discounts itself based on destroyed count.

Finisher:
    Large card that cares about total destroyed cards.

Bridge to Corporate:
    Card with text worth copying or stealing.

Bridge to Black Market:
    Created or temporary card that destroys itself.

Counter:
    Card that punishes opponent for destroying too much.

------------------------------------------------------------
3.3 Avoid
------------------------------------------------------------

Avoid too many unconditional destroy effects.
Avoid destroy cards that are also above-rate Power.
Avoid making all Destroy cards self-contained.

============================================================
4. ARCHETYPE: CORPORATE COPY
============================================================

Core domain:
    copied text

Secondary domains:
    destroyed card records
    Corp tag
    cards with copied text

Mechanical identity:
    Cards become abilities.

Player question:
    Whose ability do I steal?

Primary timing/action words:
    On Reveal
    Ongoing
    Destroy
    Copy
    Remove text

------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Package Slots
------------------------------------------------------------

Recommended launch slots:
    2 copy enablers
    2 safe copy targets
    2 payoffs for copied text
    1 finisher
    1 bridge to Destroy
    1 bridge to Nanotech / AI
    1 beginner card
    1 counter/anti-copy card

Total:
    8-10 cards

------------------------------------------------------------
4.2 Required Design Functions
------------------------------------------------------------

Enabler A:
    Destroy another card here and copy limited text.

Enabler B:
    Copy text from a card destroyed this turn.

Safe Target A:
    Simple On Reveal text that is valuable but not broken when copied.

Safe Target B:
    Simple Ongoing text that creates a clear copied-text build.

Payoff A:
    Buff cards with copied text.

Payoff B:
    Reduce Cost or gain Power when copying text.

Finisher:
    One major copy effect with strict limits.

Bridge to Destroy:
    Card that wants to be destroyed and copied.

Bridge to Nanotech:
    Ongoing/AI card that works as copy fuel.

Counter:
    Remove copied text or punish copied-text cards.

------------------------------------------------------------
4.3 Hard Constraints
------------------------------------------------------------

Do not allow unlimited text stacking at launch.

Preferred constraints:
    copy only On Reveal text
    copy only Ongoing text
    replace previous copied text
    copy only from friendly cards
    copy only from cards destroyed this turn
    remove copied text after it triggers

============================================================
5. ARCHETYPE: FIXER
============================================================

Core domain:
    hand
    deck
    tags
    created cards

Secondary domains:
    cost bands
    created-card flags
    card replacement

Mechanical identity:
    Bad options become better options.

Player question:
    Who do I call?

Primary timing/action words:
    On Reveal
    Draw
    Add
    Replace
    Reduce Cost

------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Package Slots
------------------------------------------------------------

Recommended launch slots:
    2 tag-draw Fixers
    2 random-add Fixers
    2 replace/smooth cards
    1 created-card payoff
    1 bridge to Black Market
    1 bridge to Nanotech / AI
    1 beginner card
    1 counter/anti-created card

Total:
    10-11 cards

------------------------------------------------------------
5.2 Required Design Functions
------------------------------------------------------------

Tag Draw A:
    Draw a Tech card from your deck.

Tag Draw B:
    Draw a card from another important tag.

Random Add A:
    Add a random 4-Cost and 5-Cost card from a tag.

Random Add B:
    Add a random low-cost card with a temporary modifier.

Replace A:
    Replace a card in your hand with one that costs 1 more.

Replace B:
    Replace lowest-Power or lowest-Cost card in hand.

Payoff:
    Created cards have a bonus.

Bridge to Black Market:
    Created card receives discount or temporary value.

Bridge to Nanotech:
    Draw or add AI / Tech / Ongoing card.

Counter:
    Punish opponent's created cards lightly.

------------------------------------------------------------
5.3 Avoid
------------------------------------------------------------

Avoid pure "Add 2 random cards" from the full card pool.
Avoid too much raw card advantage.
Avoid making Fixers above-rate bodies.

Fixer strength should be consistency, not raw Power.

============================================================
6. ARCHETYPE: NANOTECH / AI
============================================================

Core domain:
    Ongoing effects

Secondary domains:
    cards in lane
    modified Power
    AI tag
    created cards

Mechanical identity:
    Board presence becomes scaling Power.

Player question:
    Can I build an engine before they shut it down?

Primary timing/action words:
    Ongoing
    End of Turn
    Gain Power
    cards here

------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Package Slots
------------------------------------------------------------

Recommended launch slots:
    2 local Ongoing buffs
    2 scaling bodies
    2 End of Turn growth cards
    1 Ongoing payoff
    1 finisher
    1 bridge to Fixer
    1 bridge to Swarm
    1 counter vulnerability card

Total:
    10 cards

------------------------------------------------------------
6.2 Required Design Functions
------------------------------------------------------------

Local Buff:
    Ongoing: Your other cards here have +1 Power.

Scaling Body:
    Ongoing: This has +1 Power for each card here.

Growth Card:
    End of Turn: Give a card here +1 Power.

Ongoing Payoff:
    Your Ongoing cards have a bonus.

Finisher:
    Big late-game card that rewards board-wide systems.

Bridge to Fixer:
    AI/Tech card that can be drawn or created.

Bridge to Swarm:
    Buffs multiple small cards.

Counter:
    Ensure Hacker Control can interact with Ongoing engines.

------------------------------------------------------------
6.3 Avoid
------------------------------------------------------------

Avoid too many global Ongoing buffs.
Avoid making Ongoing decks impossible to interact with.
Avoid hidden scaling that players cannot track.

7. ARCHETYPE: HACKER CONTROL
============================================================

Core domain:
    enemy text
    enemy board
    disabled effects

Secondary domains:
    enemy hand
    enemy created cards
    modified Power

Mechanical identity:
    Opponent's plan becomes unreliable.

Player question:
    How do I interrupt their engine?

Primary timing/action words:
    On Reveal
    Ongoing
    Remove text
    Disable
    Reset Power
    Move

------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 Package Slots
------------------------------------------------------------

Recommended launch slots:
    2 Ongoing hate cards
    2 On Reveal denial cards
    1 Power reset card
    1 movement/displacement card
    1 anti-created card
    1 finisher/control payoff
    1 beginner card
    1 advanced prediction card

Total:
    8-10 cards

------------------------------------------------------------
7.2 Required Design Functions
------------------------------------------------------------

Ongoing Hate:
    Remove or suppress Ongoing text.

On Reveal Hate:
    Prevent or punish On Reveal effects at a location.

Power Reset:
    Reset modified Power.

Displacement:
    Move an enemy card.

Anti-Created:
    Punish cards that did not start in the opponent's deck.

Control Payoff:
    Gain Power if enemy cards are disabled or reduced.

Advanced Card:
    Reward guessing where the opponent plays.

------------------------------------------------------------
7.3 Avoid
------------------------------------------------------------

Avoid hard-locking the opponent.
Avoid disabling too many cards at once.
Avoid control cards with high Power.
Avoid effects that remove agency for multiple turns.

============================================================
8. ARCHETYPE: BLACK MARKET
============================================================

Core domain:
    created cards
    discounts
    temporary value

Secondary domains:
    self-destruction
    destroyed cards
    cost modification

Mechanical identity:
    Future stability becomes immediate tempo.

Player question:
    Can I win before the bill comes due?

Primary timing/action words:
    Add
    Reduce Cost
    Destroy
    next turn
    End of Turn

------------------------------------------------------------
8.1 Package Slots
------------------------------------------------------------

Recommended launch slots:
    2 discount enablers
    2 temporary created-card generators
    2 risk/payoff cards
    1 self-destruct bridge to Destroy
    1 bridge to Fixer
    1 finisher
    1 counter/anti-discount card

Total:
    10 cards

------------------------------------------------------------
8.2 Required Design Functions
------------------------------------------------------------

Discount Enabler A:
    Reduce the Cost of a created card.

Discount Enabler B:
    Add a random card that costs less next turn only.

Temporary Card A:
    Created card destroys itself later.

Temporary Card B:
    Created card has a downside if held too long.

Risk Payoff:
    Gain Power when you play a discounted card.

Self-Destruct Bridge:
    Temporary card fuels Destroy.

Fixer Bridge:
    Fixer creates the card; Black Market discounts it.

Finisher:
    Big tempo swing based on discounts or created cards.

Counter:
    Punish heavily discounted or created cards.

------------------------------------------------------------
8.3 Avoid
------------------------------------------------------------

Avoid permanent large discounts with no downside.
Avoid adding too many cards from the full pool.
Avoid making random generation stronger than deckbuilding.

9. ARCHETYPE: OVERCLOCK
============================================================

Core domain:
    unspent Energy

Secondary domains:
    played no cards last turn
    spent all Energy last turn
    delayed payoff

Mechanical identity:
    Restraint becomes power.

Player question:
    Should I hold back now to hit harder later?

Primary timing/action words:
    End of Turn
    unspent Energy
    last turn
    costs less
    gain Power

------------------------------------------------------------
9.1 Package Slots
------------------------------------------------------------

Recommended launch slots:
    2 unspent Energy payoffs
    2 delayed discount cards
    2 cards that reward playing no cards or fewer cards
    1 finisher
    1 bridge to Black Market
    1 bridge to Nanotech / AI
    1 beginner card
    1 counter/pressure card

Total:
    8-10 cards

------------------------------------------------------------
9.2 Required Design Functions
------------------------------------------------------------

Payoff A:
    If you ended last turn with unspent Energy, gain Power.

Payoff B:
    End of Turn: If you have unspent Energy, grow.

Discount Card A:
    Costs less if you had unspent Energy last turn.

Low-Play Reward:
    Reward playing no cards or one card.

Finisher:
    Converts accumulated restraint into a late swing.

Bridge to Black Market:
    Discounted card or temporary spike.

Bridge to Nanotech:
    Ongoing card that grows while Energy is unspent.

Counter:
    Pressure card that punishes slow starts.

------------------------------------------------------------
9.3 Avoid
------------------------------------------------------------

Avoid making skipping turns always correct.
Avoid huge payoff for doing nothing without risk.
Avoid too many "played no cards" effects at launch.

============================================================
15A. SECONDARY PACKAGE: BANISH / PERMANENT REMOVAL
============================================================

Core domain:
    banished cards

Launch size:
    0-3 cards, only if needed

Mechanical identity:
    Some threats are moved to a final gameplay zone.

Required functions if used:
    1 narrow banish effect
    1 card that counts banished cards
    1 anti-recursion tool

Allowed:
    Banish a card.
    Count banished cards.
    Trigger when banished.

Forbidden:
    Recover from banish.
    Return from banish.
    Move from banish.
    Regenerate from banish.
    Copy from banish as a recovery workaround.

Launch warning:
    Banish is much more severe than destroy or discard because players cannot
    recover from it. It should be rare, visible, and expensive.

16. CROSS-ARCHETYPE BRIDGE MAP
============================================================

Destroy bridges to:
    Corporate Copy via destroyed text targets.
    Black Market via self-destruct temporary cards.
    Swarm via cheap disposable bodies.

Corporate Copy bridges to:
    Destroy via sacrifice.
    Nanotech via copied Ongoing text.
    Hacker Control via text manipulation.

Fixer bridges to:
    Black Market via created cards and discounts.
    Nanotech via Tech/AI draw.
    Swarm via created bodies.
    Military midrange via 4-Cost and 5-Cost generation.

Nanotech bridges to:
    Swarm via lane-wide buffs.
    Biohack via modified Power.
    Corporate Copy via Ongoing text worth copying.

Hacker Control bridges to:
    Surveillance via enemy hand/play pattern information.
    Malware via enemy deck sabotage.
    Corporate Copy via text removal/copy counterplay.

Black Market bridges to:
    Fixer via created cards.
    Destroy via self-destruct cards.
    Overclock via delayed tempo windows.
    Biohack via discounted upgrades.

Overclock bridges to:
    Black Market via timed discounts.
    Nanotech via End of Turn growth.
    Control via holding back and reacting.

Swarm bridges to:
    Destroy via fodder.
    Nanotech via board-wide buffs.
    Fixer via created cheap bodies.

Runner bridges to:
    Hacker Control via enemy displacement.
    Swarm via filling lanes dynamically.
    Overclock via delayed repositioning.

Biohack bridges to:
    Nanotech via Power modification.
    Black Market via illegal upgrades.
    Swarm via buffing cheap bodies.

Surveillance bridges to:
    Hacker Control via enemy information.
    Malware via future sabotage.
    Overclock via prediction and restraint.

Malware bridges to:
    Surveillance via enemy resource knowledge.
    Hacker Control via disruption.
    Black Market via illegal corrupted cards.

============================================================
19. FINAL DESIGN RULE
============================================================

Do not ask:
    "What is a cool cyberpunk card?"

Ask:
    "What package slot does this card fill, what tracked variable does it care
    about, and what other archetype can also use it?"

Persistent entity correction:
    Destroy, discard, and banish are zone changes.
    Banish is final in gameplay and should never have recovery effects.

End of Launch Archetype Packages v0.2.
