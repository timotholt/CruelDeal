========================================================================
0. DESIGN NOTE
========================================================================

This is the first actual card pass after the Domain Bible and Archetype Package spec.

Rules text is intentionally simple and factual.
Lore appears in names, tags, and archetype grouping.

Balance is provisional.
Costs and Power are first-pass placeholders for playtesting.


Engine correction:
    Cards are persistent entities.
    Destroy means move to destroyed pile / death pile.
    Discard means move to discard pile.
    Banish means move to banish pile.
    No card should recover cards from the banish pile.

Assumptions:
    3 lanes
    6-turn Energy curve
    12-card decks
    small hand size with created-card flags
    cards can be Characters or Objects

Launch card count:
    105 cards

4. FIRST-PASS BALANCE FLAGS
========================================================================

- 018 Executive Override: Full-text copying is dangerous and may need to become On Reveal-only or Ongoing-only.
- 021 Military Fixer: Adding two cards may be strong, especially if the Military pool has premium 4/5-cost cards.
- 025 Procurement Bot: Ongoing Cost reduction for all created cards in hand may need a once-per-turn cap.
- 039 Signal Jammer: Persistent On Reveal denial can hard-counter too much if placed cheaply.
- 046 Null Saint: Removing all enemy text here is a huge swing and may need lower Power or higher Cost.
- 047 Black Market Dealer: Full-pool random discount must be controlled by curated pools or tags.
- 056 Kingpin Broker: Discount payoff can snowball if cost reduction is too common.
- 062 Silent Engine: Repeated Ongoing buffs may create runaway Nanotech/Overclock hybrids.
- 073 Gutter Legion: Full-location bonuses may be too easy in Swarm.
- 088 Panopticon AI: Enemy added-to-hand penalty can feel oppressive if many decks create cards.
- 089 Junk Packet: Enemy deck junk must have clear visual feedback.
- 105 Hard Reset: Full lane reset is powerful anti-buff tech and may define the meta.
