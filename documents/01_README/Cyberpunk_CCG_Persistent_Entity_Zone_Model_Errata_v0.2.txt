CYBERPUNK CCG — PERSISTENT ENTITY / ZONE MODEL ERRATA v0.2
Created: 2026-04-23

This errata corrects the v0.1 design docs.

============================================================
1. CORE CORRECTION
============================================================

Cards are never destroyed from the system.

Cards are persistent entities. They always continue to exist as card objects
with identity, ownership, history, current zone, and metadata.

The game terms Destroy, Discard, and Banish are player-facing words for zone
changes.

============================================================
2. CORRECT DEFINITIONS
============================================================

Destroy:
    Move a card from board/lane to the destroyed pile / death pile.

Discard:
    Move a card from hand to the discard pile.

Banish:
    Move a card to the banish pile.

Draw:
    Move a card from deck to hand.

Play:
    Move a card from hand to board/lane.

Return:
    Move a card from one zone back to another legal zone, usually hand, deck,
    or board.

============================================================
3. BANISH DESIGN RULE
============================================================

Banished cards still exist in the engine.

However:

    No card should ever pull from the banish pile.
    No card should recover from the banish pile.
    No card should regenerate from the banish pile.
    No card should move a card out of the banish pile.
    No card should copy a banished card as a recovery workaround.

Banish is persistent in the engine but final in gameplay.

Allowed:
    Count banished cards.
    Trigger when cards are banished.
    Record banished-card history.
    Use banish as permanent gameplay removal.

============================================================
4. ENGINE FIELDS
============================================================

Every card entity should support:

    card_instance_id
    base_card_id
    owner_player_id
    controller_player_id
    current_zone
    current_lane
    previous_zone
    previous_lane
    zone_history
    turn_entered_current_zone
    original_cost
    current_cost
    original_power
    current_power
    original_text
    current_text
    copied_text_records
    disabled_text_state
    tags
    was_created
    was_destroyed_this_game
    was_discarded_this_game
    was_banished_this_game
    last_zone_change_reason

Every zone change should create a record.
